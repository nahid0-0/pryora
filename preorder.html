QUESTIONS

Before you reserve

When do I pay?

Only when your piece is delivered. Pay the courier in cash or by card. There is no advance and no deposit.

What if the ship date changes?

We will message you straight away with the new date. If it no longer works for you, we cancel the reservation, no questions asked.

Can I change the size or cancel?

Yes, any time before it ships. Reply to our confirmation message or call us with your reservation number.

Is a pre-order piece the same as the shop piece?

Exactly the same. Pre-order only means the batch is still being finished. It comes in the same Pryora pouch and box.
